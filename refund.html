EZ-Fin Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Operated by: Clint Proctor, a sole proprietor trading as EZ-Fin ("EZ-Fin", "we", "us")

Address: 33 Beach Road, Westbrook, Tongaat, 4439

Email: admin@ez-fin.co.za

Contact Number: 061 307 8212

This policy explains how you can cancel your EZ-Fin subscription and when you can get a refund. It applies to the EZ-Fin web app at app.ez-fin.co.za. It does not limit any rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002.

1. Free trial

  • Every new account gets a 30-day free trial. No card details are needed to start.

  • Nothing is charged during or after the trial unless you choose a plan and pay.

  • If you do not pay when the trial ends, your account becomes view-only for 10 days, and is then suspended. You will not owe us anything.

  • If you pay during the trial, you keep the trial days you have left. Your paid period starts the day after your trial ends.

2. Plans and payment

  • Monthly plan: R50 per month.

  • Annual plan: R500 per year.

  • Prices include VAT where applicable and may change. We will give you at least 30 days' notice by email before a price change affects you.

  • Payments are made once per period through our payment partner, using a secure payment link. We do not debit your card automatically. A new period only starts when you choose to pay.

  • We email you reminders before your trial ends and before each renewal date.

3. How to cancel

Because there are no automatic debits, the simplest way to cancel is to not pay for the next period. Your access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for.

If you would like us to close your account, or you are asking for a refund, email admin@ez-fin.co.za from the email address on your account, with:

  1. Your name and account number (for example MF-00012).

  2. Whether you want to cancel, get a refund, or both.

  3. Whether you want your data deleted (see section 6).

We will confirm by email within 2 business days.

4. Refunds

4.1 7-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy, you can cancel within 7 days of any payment and get a full refund of that payment, with no reason needed. This matches the cooling-off right in section 44 of the ECT Act.

4.2 Monthly plan after 7 days

After the 7 days, monthly payments are not refunded. Your access continues until the end of the month you paid for, and nothing further is charged.

4.3 Annual plan after 7 days

If you cancel an Annual plan after the first 7 days, we refund the unused full months at R500 ÷ 12 per month (rounded down to the nearest rand). The month in which you cancel is not refunded. Your access ends on the date we process the refund.

4.4 When a refund may not apply

  • Accounts closed by us because of fraud, abuse, or a serious breach of our Terms of Use.

  • Payments older than 12 months.

4.5 Our mistakes

If you were charged in error (for example, a double payment), or EZ-Fin was unavailable for a long time because of a fault on our side, we will refund the affected amount in full.

5. How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the card or account you paid with, through our payment partner only.

  • We start the refund within 5 business days of approving it. Your bank may take a further 5 to 10 business days to show it.

  • All refunds are in South African rand (ZAR). We are not responsible for bank or exchange-rate charges on your side.

  • You will get an email confirming the refund and a credit note for your records.

6. What happens to your data when you cancel

  • When your paid period or trial ends, your account becomes view-only for 10 days, so you can still look at your information. After that it is suspended.

  • If you come back and pay within 90 days, your data will still be there.

  • After 90 days of suspension or cancellation, we permanently delete your financial data (transactions, budgets, accounts and similar).

  • You can ask us to delete your data sooner. Once deleted, it cannot be recovered.

  • We must keep invoices and payment records for 5 years for tax purposes, even after your account is closed.

See our Privacy Policy for more detail.

7. Extra users

An account can have up to 3 logins sharing one set of data. Only the account Owner can cancel the account or ask for a refund. Removing an extra user does not change the price or lead to a refund.

8. If we cancel or end the service

  • We may suspend an account that breaks our Terms of Use. Where possible we will warn you first.

  • If we stop offering EZ-Fin, we will give you at least 30 days' notice, a chance to export your data, and a pro-rata refund of any unused paid period.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled a cancellation or refund, email admin@ez-fin.co.za. We aim to resolve complaints within 10 business days. If you are still not satisfied, you may contact the National Consumer Commission (www.thencc.gov.za) or the Consumer Goods and Services Ombud (www.cgso.org.za).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version, with its effective date, will always be available on our website. Changes will not affect payments already made.
