Frequently asked questions

Do I need any accounting knowledge?

No. EZ-Fin is built for everyday households. If you can read a bank statement, you can use EZ-Fin.

Which banks can I import from?

Any bank that lets you download statements as CSV, Excel or PDF — which covers all the major South African banks.

Is my data safe?

Your data is synced securely and only visible to you when you sign in. EZ-Fin never connects to your bank or moves money.

Can my partner and I both use it?

Yes. Add each person in your household and filter or compare by person.

Does it help with tax?

EZ-Fin tracks deductions and tax-claimable items across the March–February tax year, so your figures are ready at filing time. It does not replace a tax practitioner.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time. Annual plans run to the end of the paid year.
