EZ-Fin Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Responsible party: Clint Proctor, a sole proprietor trading as EZ-Fin ("EZ-Fin", "we", "us")

Information Officer: Clint Proctor (owner)

Address: 33 Beach Road, Westbrook, Tongaat, 4439

Contact: admin@ez-fin.co.za

EZ-Fin is a household finance manager. You trust us with personal and financial information, and we take that seriously. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who this policy applies to

It applies to everyone who visits ez-fin.co.za, signs up for a trial, pays for a plan, or is invited as an extra user on an account.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account details (you give us)

  • Required: first name, last name, email address, password, and your POPIA consent (with the date and time you gave it).

  • Optional: mobile number, country, province or region, how you heard about us, and whether you want marketing emails.

  • Your password is stored in encrypted (hashed) form. We cannot see it.

2.2 Financial information (you enter in the app)

Transactions, income, expenses, budgets, bank account names and balances, savings, tax information and any notes you add. You decide what to enter. We do not connect to your bank and never ask for internet banking login details.

2.3 Cash slip photos

If you use the cash slip feature, the photo is read on your own device to fill in the shop, date and amount. The photo is never uploaded or saved. Only the details you choose to save are kept.

2.4 Payment information

Payments are handled by our approved payment partner. We never see or store your card number. We keep the plan, amount, date, payment reference, invoice number and whether the payment succeeded.

2.5 Technical information

When you use the app or website we may collect your IP address, browser and device type, login times and basic error logs. These help us keep the service secure and working.

2.6 Emails we send you

We keep a record of the service emails we send (for example trial and renewal reminders and invites), including the date and whether they were delivered.

3. Why we use your information

Purpose Lawful basis under POPIA
Create and run your account, and keep your data in sync across your logins Needed to perform our agreement with you
Take payments, issue invoices and send renewal reminders Agreement with you; legal duty (tax records)
Keep the service secure and fix problems Our legitimate interest
Answer support questions Agreement with you
Send news, tips and offers Only with your consent (opt-in). You can unsubscribe at any time
Understand how people find us (referral source) and improve EZ-Fin, using combined, anonymous figures Our legitimate interest

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions about you that have legal effects.

4. Who can see your information

4.1 Inside EZ-Fin

  • Your financial data: only you and the users on your account. We cannot read your financial data; the system is built this way on purpose.

  • Account and payment details: the owner of EZ-Fin and anyone he authorises to help, to manage accounts, payments and support.

4.2 Shared accounts

An account can have up to 3 logins that share one set of data. The account Owner adds or removes extra users. Anyone the Owner adds can see and change the shared financial data. Only invite people you trust.

4.4 When the law requires it

We may share information if a court order, law or regulator requires it, or to protect someone's safety or prevent fraud.

5. Information sent outside South Africa

Some providers store or process information outside South Africa (see the table above). Under section 72 of POPIA, we only use providers in countries with data-protection laws similar to POPIA (such as the UK and EU) or who are bound by contracts giving the same level of protection. By agreeing to this policy, you consent to these transfers.

6. How we protect your information

  • All information travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

  • Passwords are hashed; payment and email keys are kept as secrets on the server and never in the app.

  • Database rules make sure each account can only reach its own data.

  • Anyone who helps us run EZ-Fin only gets access to what they need for their task.

  • If a security breach puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as soon as reasonably possible, as POPIA requires.

You help too: use a strong password, do not share your login, and log out on shared devices.

7. How long we keep it

Information How long
Account and financial data While your account is active or in trial
After cancellation or suspension Kept for 90 days so you can return, then permanently deleted
Invoices and payment records 5 years, as tax law requires
Email logs and technical logs Up to 12 months
Marketing consent records Until you withdraw consent, plus 12 months as proof

You can ask us to delete your data sooner (see section 8). Information in backups is overwritten within a short period after deletion.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  1. Ask what information we hold about you and get a copy.

  2. Correct information that is wrong or out of date. You can update most details yourself in the app under Account.

  3. Ask us to delete your information (subject to what the law requires us to keep).

  4. Object to us using your information, or withdraw consent (for example, to marketing emails).

  5. Complain to the Information Regulator.

To use any of these rights, email admin@ez-fin.co.za from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within 30 days, and there is no charge for reasonable requests.

9. Marketing emails

We only send marketing emails if you ticked the opt-in box. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link. Service emails (such as trial reminders, renewal reminders and invoices) are part of running your account and are not marketing.

10. Cookies and browser storage

The EZ-Fin app uses your browser's storage only to keep you logged in and remember your settings. The app does not use advertising cookies. Our marketing website may use analytics or advertising tools (for example from Google or Meta) to measure how our adverts perform; you can block these in your browser settings.

11. Children

EZ-Fin is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are important, we will email you before they take effect. The current version will always be on our website with its effective date.
